Student Exercises

Here are some exercises you can do to help nail down the concepts we have discussed. Some are more difficult than others. All of them have been tested in real-life situations with people like you. I have included exercises from previous editions of this book that I developed in the courses I have taught. I have dusted them off and gotten rid of any “cobwebs,” so that you have clear exercises to work with. Several of the activities come from Dr. Robert Permenter, a former student of mine at Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary. Bob is a pastor who has taught preaching courses at Bethel Seminary, and I appreciate his contribution here. Other exercises come from Scott Wenig, editor of the fourth edition, who also holds the Haddon Robinson Chair of Biblical Preaching at Denver Seminary.

Working through these exercises can be both interesting and frustrating. After all, thinking can be hard work. Completing them, however, should help you better grasp and apply the material presented in Biblical Preaching.
