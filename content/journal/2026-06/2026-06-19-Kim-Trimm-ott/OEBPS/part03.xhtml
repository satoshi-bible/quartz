Part 3

CONTEXT

Although the approaches we describe in part 3 differ from one another in substantial ways, they all share a focus on the importance of context. Chapter 5, “Canonical Old Testament Theology,” examines theologies that see the Christian canon as the proper context for Old Testament theology and therefore highlight connections with the New Testament. By contrast, chapter 6, “Jewish Biblical Theology,” covers theologies that read the Hebrew Bible from the context of a Jewish background. Finally, chapter 7, “Postmodern Old Testament Theology,” considers theologies that emphasize the particular contexts of individual interpreters and interpretive communities, while also pointing out the significant diversity found within the Old Testament.
